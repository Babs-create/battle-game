Bag

    Tap a weapon to equip it. You fight with your equipped weapon in battles, and every hit you land wears it down. At 0 durability it breaks. Bows and slingshots shoot from range. Any ranged weapon (bows, crossbows, slingshots) halves your walking speed. In battle, press Q to dodge roll: you cannot be hit mid-roll. Win battles in a row to face tougher rivals that pay more. Spend coins in the Shop on new weapons and repairs, or save up to buy new islands from the Islands menu. Each one costs more than the last and hides stronger weapons, up to Mythic.
